Shottr and Ledge both make screenshots on a Mac less painful, but they solve different problems. Shottr is a **capture and annotation tool**: it replaces how you take screenshots and adds power tools. Ledge is a **screenshot shelf**: it keeps the screenshots you already take within reach, with a quick editor for markup. Many people will be happy with one; some will want both.

## At a glance

| | Ledge | Shottr |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A shelf for recent screenshots at the top of the screen | A screenshot capture and annotation app |
| How you capture | The normal macOS shortcuts | Shottr's own capture shortcuts |
| Recent screenshots | Shelf of your last 24, one gesture away | Saved to a folder you choose |
| Annotation | Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text | Arrows, text, shapes, highlights, spotlights, step counters |
| Hide sensitive info | Pixelate tool | Pixelate and remove objects |
| Power features | Drag into any app, keyboard-driven, Tidy Desktop | Scrolling capture, OCR, QR reading, screen ruler, color picker, pinned screenshots |
| Uploads | None, everything stays on your Mac | Optional S3-compatible upload |
| Network access | None | Used for license activation and update checks |
| Price | Free | Free to use; optional name-your-price license unlocks uploads and some options |
| Source code | Open source (MIT) | Closed source |

## Where Shottr is stronger

Shottr is one of the most capable capture tools on the Mac. If you need **scrolling screenshots** of long pages, **OCR** to pull text out of an image, a **pixel ruler** to measure UI, or a **color picker** with contrast checks, Shottr does all of that and Ledge does not. It is also very small and fast.

## Where Ledge is stronger

Ledge does not change how you take screenshots. You keep using `Shift` `Cmd` `4`, and every capture lands on a shelf that slides down when you rest the pointer at the top of any screen. That makes a few things much easier:

- **Getting back to recent screenshots.** Your last 24 captures are always one gesture or `Cmd` `Option` `L` away, not buried in a folder.
- **Dragging them into other apps.** Pull a screenshot straight into Slack, Mail, Messages or a Finder window.
- **Keeping your Desktop clear.** Turn on Move New Screenshots into Ledge and old captures go to the Trash as you take them off the shelf.
- **Privacy.** Ledge has no account, no analytics and makes no network requests at all.
- **Openness.** Ledge is free and open source under the MIT license, so you can read exactly what it does.

## Can you use both?

Yes, and it works well. If Shottr saves its captures to a folder, point Ledge at the same folder from its menu bar icon (**Choose Folder…**). Shottr handles the capturing and power tools; Ledge keeps the results on the shelf, ready to drag.

## Which should you choose?

- Choose **Shottr** if you need scrolling capture, OCR, measurements or color tools.
- Choose **Ledge** if your screenshots get lost on the Desktop and you mostly capture, mark up and send.
- Use **both** if you want Shottr's capture tools and a shelf to grab results from.
