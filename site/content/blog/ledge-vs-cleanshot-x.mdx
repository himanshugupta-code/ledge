CleanShot X is one of the best-known screenshot apps on the Mac: a complete suite for capturing, recording, annotating and sharing. Ledge is much smaller and does one job: it keeps the screenshots you take on a shelf at the top of your screen, with a quick editor. So is Ledge a free CleanShot X alternative? For some people, yes. For others, not at all. Here is how to tell.

## At a glance

| | Ledge | CleanShot X |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A shelf for recent screenshots | A full capture, recording and sharing suite |
| How you capture | The normal macOS shortcuts | CleanShot's own capture tools |
| Screen recording | No | Yes |
| Scrolling capture | No | Yes |
| Annotation | Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, pixelate | Extensive built-in annotation tool |
| Recent screenshots | Shelf of your last 24 at the top of the screen | Quick Access overlay after each capture |
| Cloud sharing | None, everything stays on your Mac | CleanShot Cloud links |
| Price | Free | Basic $35 once (1 year of updates, then optional $19/year); Pro $10 per user per month, billed annually |
| Trial | Not needed | No trial; 30-day money-back guarantee, or via Setapp's trial |
| Source code | Open source (MIT) | Closed source |

CleanShot X pricing is from [cleanshot.com/pricing](https://cleanshot.com/pricing) in October 2026 and may change.

## When CleanShot X is worth paying for

If screenshots and screen recordings are a big part of your work, CleanShot X is excellent. **Screen recording**, **scrolling capture**, a rich annotation tool and **cloud links** you can paste anywhere are things Ledge simply does not do. Teams that share captures all day often get their money's worth.

## When Ledge is enough

A lot of people buy a capture suite when what actually annoys them is simpler: screenshots disappear onto a cluttered Desktop, and finding the one you took ten minutes ago takes longer than taking it. If that is you, Ledge may be all you need:

- Keep using the macOS shortcuts you already know.
- Rest the pointer at the top edge, or press `Cmd` `Option` `L`, to see your recent screenshots.
- Drag one into Slack, Mail or a document, or copy it with one click.
- Click it to add arrows and text or **pixelate** sensitive details.
- Turn on Move New Screenshots into Ledge to keep your Desktop clear.

And it costs nothing, has no account, and never sends your screenshots anywhere.

## Using them together

If you already own CleanShot X, you can still use Ledge as a shelf. Point Ledge at the folder CleanShot saves to (**Choose Folder…** in Ledge's menu bar icon) and your CleanShot captures land on the shelf too.

## The short answer

- You need recording, scrolling capture or cloud links: **CleanShot X**.
- You need your screenshots out of the way but one reach away, with quick markup: **Ledge**, free.
