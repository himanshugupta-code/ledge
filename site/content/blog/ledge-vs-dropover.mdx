"Shelf" apps are a favourite Mac utility: a temporary place to park things while you move them between apps. Dropover is one of the best-known. Ledge also calls itself a shelf, but it is built for one kind of item: your screenshots. The difference matters more than it sounds.

## At a glance

| | Ledge | Dropover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What goes on the shelf | Your screenshots, automatically | Anything you drag onto it: files, images, links, text |
| How it opens | Rest the pointer at the top edge, or `Cmd` `Option` `L` | Shake the pointer while dragging, or a shortcut |
| Fills itself | Yes, every new screenshot lands on it | With watched folders |
| Editing | Built-in editor: annotate, pixelate, adjust, crop | File actions such as resizing images and extracting text |
| Sharing | Drag out or copy | Drag out, Share menu, AirDrop, and uploads to cloud services |
| Network access | None | Optional cloud uploads |
| Price | Free and open source | Available on the Mac App Store |

Dropover details are from [dropoverapp.com](https://dropoverapp.com/) in October 2026; check the App Store listing for current pricing.

## Where Dropover shines

Dropover is a general-purpose drag-and-drop tool. If you regularly gather **files from several places**, move them as a batch, zip them, resize images or **upload to cloud storage**, Dropover is built for exactly that, with scripting and automation for power users.

## Where Ledge shines

Ledge assumes the thing you keep reaching for is a screenshot. So it does not wait for you to put anything on the shelf:

- **Every screenshot appears on it automatically**, newest first.
- **It is always in the same place.** Move the pointer to the top edge of any screen and it slides down.
- **You can fix a screenshot before sending it:** add arrows and text, pixelate a password, crop or adjust it.
- **It can keep your Desktop clear** by moving new captures into its own folder.
- **It is private:** free, open source, no account, and no network requests.

## Use both

They do not compete for the same job. Ledge holds your screenshots at the top of the screen; Dropover holds whatever you happen to be dragging around. Plenty of people will happily run both.

## Which should you pick?

- You move all kinds of files between apps and want cloud uploads: **Dropover**.
- Your problem is finding and sharing recent screenshots: **Ledge**.
