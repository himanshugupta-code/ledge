## Promotional text
Your newest screenshots wait along the top of the screen, out of the way until you reach for them.

## Description
Ledge keeps your latest screenshots on a slim shelf at the top edge of your screen.

Take a screenshot as usual. It appears on the ledge. Move your pointer to the top edge, or press Cmd+Option+L, and the shelf slides into view. Drag a screenshot straight into any app, copy it to the clipboard, open it, or reveal it in Finder.

- Reveals when you reach the top edge, hides when you move away
- Peeks briefly when a new screenshot arrives
- Drag and drop into Messages, Mail, Slack, docs and more
- One-click copy to clipboard
- Optional: move new screenshots off your Desktop into Ledge
- Lives in the menu bar, no Dock icon
- Private: everything stays on your Mac. No account, no network, no tracking.

On first launch, choose the folder where your screenshots are saved. Ledge only looks at that folder.

## Review notes (for App Review)
Ledge is a menu bar app (LSUIElement) with no Dock icon and no main window. After launch, a folder picker asks the user to choose their screenshot folder (macOS default: Desktop). Take a screenshot with Cmd+Shift+3 and move the pointer to the top edge of the screen, or press Cmd+Option+L, to show the shelf. No login or account is required.

## App Privacy
Data collected: **None**. The app makes no network requests and has no analytics.
